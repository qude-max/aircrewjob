Spatial Orientation NEW

Heading 240°, beacon bears 135° — where is it relative to your nose? The classic compass-reasoning task, 60 seconds against the clock.

Instrument Scan NEW

Six live instruments, needles wandering. One drifts out of the green band — catch it fast. Pure monitoring, like the real screening task.

Memory Grid NEW

Cells flash in sequence on a 4×4 grid — replay them in order. Visual-spatial memory, the cut-e matrix format.

Reaction & Vigilance

Wait for the disc to turn cyan, then hit it — 8 rounds with random delays. Jump early and you're penalised.

🧠

Memory Span

A digit sequence flashes once, then you type it back. Each pass adds a digit — forwards recall under time pressure.

🎛️

Multitask Monitor

Mental arithmetic while monitoring six warning gauges — divided attention under time pressure, COMPASS-style.
