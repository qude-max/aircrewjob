Train like it's assessment day

Airline screening (cut-e/AON, CAE, COMPASS) measures reaction, memory and divided attention. These trainers exercise exactly those muscles — scores stay on your device.

⚡

Reaction & Vigilance

Wait for the disc to turn cyan, then hit it — 8 rounds with random delays. Jump early and you're penalised, exactly like a vigilance task.

🧠

Memory Span

A digit sequence flashes once, then you type it back. Each pass adds a digit. Airlines test forwards and backwards recall — this trains both.

🎛️

Multitask Monitor

Keep answering mental-arithmetic questions while monitoring six gauges — when one goes red, silence it fast. Divided attention under time pressure.

These trainers build the underlying skills but are not replicas of any provider's copyrighted tests. For airline-specific formats, practise the official familiarisation material the airline sends you — and read our assessment prep guide.
