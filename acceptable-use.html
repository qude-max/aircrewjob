This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") sets out the rules, standards, and obligations governing the use of the Platform at www.aircrewjob.com (the "Platform") by all Users. This AUP forms part of and must be read together with the Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy, and Cookie Policy published on the Platform, all of which are incorporated by reference herein. Capitalised terms used but not defined in this AUP have the meanings given to them in the Terms and Conditions.

By accessing or using the Platform, you agree to be bound by this AUP in addition to the Terms and Conditions. If you do not agree to this AUP, you must immediately cease using the Platform.

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 The Platform is designed exclusively to serve the global aviation community by connecting Aircrew with Recruiters, providing access to a curated database of aviation industry resources, and facilitating professional networking among aviation professionals. The standards set out in this AUP exist to maintain the integrity, safety, and professional character of the Platform and to protect the rights and interests of all Users.

1.2 This AUP applies to all Users of the Platform, including Aircrew, Recruiters, visitors, and any other person or entity accessing any feature or functionality of the Platform, whether registered or unregistered.

1.3 This AUP also applies to your conduct on or in connection with the Platform-linked Discord server, to the extent that such conduct relates to or arises from your participation in the Platform community.

1.4 The Operator reserves the right to update this AUP at any time. Updated versions will be published on the Platform with a revised effective date. Your continued use of the Platform following any amendment constitutes your acceptance of the revised AUP.

2. PERMITTED USES

2.1 You are permitted to use the Platform for the following purposes only:

(a) creating and maintaining an Aircrew profile to represent your professional aviation credentials, qualifications, and employment history accurately and in good faith;

(b) searching, browsing, and applying for genuine aviation employment opportunities listed on the Platform;

(c) searching for and contacting Aircrew for the purposes of genuine, lawful aviation recruitment, as a Recruiter;

(d) posting genuine, accurate, and lawful job listings as a Recruiter on behalf of a legitimate aviation employer or operator;

(e) accessing the Platform's database of airlines, flight schools, and aviation medical examiners for legitimate informational and professional reference purposes;

(f) submitting genuine, honest, and first-hand reviews and ratings of flight schools you have attended or with which you have had direct professional experience;

(g) communicating and networking with other registered Users for professional aviation purposes; and

(h) such other uses as the Operator expressly authorises in writing from time to time.

3. CREDENTIAL INTEGRITY AND PROFESSIONAL ACCURACY

3.1 Given the safety-critical nature of the aviation industry, the Operator treats credential integrity as a matter of the highest importance. All Users are strictly prohibited from:

(a) submitting, posting, or representing any aviation licence, rating, endorsement, type rating, total flight hours, or any other professional credential that is false, fabricated, exaggerated, expired, suspended, revoked, or otherwise inaccurate;

(b) misrepresenting their qualifications, employment history, or aviation experience in any manner, including through omission of material information;

(c) claiming to hold a position, rank, or designation that they do not hold or are not currently authorised to exercise;

(d) representing credentials as their own that belong to another individual; or

(e) failing to update their profile promptly following any suspension, revocation, restriction, or material change to any licence, rating, endorsement, or medical certificate.

3.2 Recruiters must not post job listings that misrepresent the nature, location, terms, or conditions of the position being advertised, or that advertise positions that do not genuinely exist.

3.3 The Operator may, but is not obligated to, take steps to verify User credentials. The absence of verification does not relieve any User of responsibility for the accuracy of their profile or listings. Users who discover inaccurate or fraudulent credentials on the Platform are encouraged to report such content to the Operator through the reporting mechanisms available on the Platform.

(c) creating or maintaining multiple Accounts without the Operator's prior written consent;

(d) sharing, selling, transferring, or otherwise providing access to your Account to any third party;

(e) posting, transmitting, or distributing any content that is defamatory, discriminatory, hateful, offensive, inflammatory, threatening, harassing, abusive, or that incites violence or discrimination on any grounds, including race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, religion, disability, age, or sexual orientation;

(g) collecting, harvesting, or otherwise extracting personal data about other Users from the Platform for any commercial, marketing, or other purpose not expressly permitted by these documents;

6.1 All Users are strictly prohibited from:

(a) using any personal data of other Users obtained through the Platform for any purpose other than the legitimate aviation recruitment or professional networking purpose for which that data was shared;

(b) selling, licensing, transferring, or otherwise commercialising the personal data of any other User obtained through the Platform;

(d) aggregating, compiling, or otherwise creating a database or dataset derived from User profiles or data obtained from the Platform for any commercial or competitive purpose; or

Recruiters must implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect such data and must not retain it beyond the period necessary for the relevant recruitment purpose.

7. REVIEW AND RATING STANDARDS

7.1 Note: flight school reviews and ratings are a planned feature and are not yet available on the Platform. The standards in this Section 7 will come into effect when that feature is launched and are included now to establish the governing framework in advance.

7.2 When available, Users who submit reviews or ratings of flight schools must comply with the following standards: (a) reviews must be based on genuine, direct, first-hand experience; (b) reviews must be truthful, fair, and accurate; (c) reviews must not be submitted by individuals who are affiliated with the reviewed school without full disclosure; (d) reviews must not contain defamatory statements; (e) reviews must not form part of a coordinated, incentivised, or artificial review campaign; (f) reviews must not include personal attacks on individual identifiable staff beyond fair comment on the training experience; and (g) reviews must not violate any other provision of this AUP or the Terms and Conditions.

7.3 Flight schools and third parties may not solicit, incentivise, or coerce Users to submit positive reviews or to withdraw negative reviews. Any such conduct should be reported to the Operator.

8.1 The Platform, including the associated Discord server, is a professional community for aviation industry participants. All Users are expected to conduct themselves with professionalism, courtesy, and respect at all times.

(b) discrimination against any individual on the basis of race, ethnicity, national origin, gender, religion, age, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic under Applicable Law;

(c) the sharing of another User's personal information without that User's consent ("doxxing");

(d) threatening, abusive, or violent communications of any kind; or

(e) any conduct that creates a hostile, unwelcoming, or unsafe environment for any other User.

8.3 The Operator is committed to maintaining an inclusive and welcoming professional environment. Reports of harassment, discrimination, or abusive conduct may be submitted to the Operator through the contact details published on the Platform and will be investigated promptly.
