1. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

1.1 Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) by a website when you visit it. Cookies are widely used by website operators to make websites work more efficiently, to provide functionality, and to collect information about how Users interact with websites.

1.2 Cookies may be set by the operator of the website you are visiting ("first-party cookies") or by third parties providing services on the website's behalf, such as analytics providers or advertising networks ("third-party cookies"). First-party cookies can only be read by the website that set them. Third-party cookies may be shared with and read by the third party that set them across multiple websites.

1.3 Cookies may be "session cookies" which expire when you close your browser, or "persistent cookies" which remain on your device for a set period of time or until you delete them. Both types of cookies are used on the Platform.

1.4 Cookies are not the only tracking technologies that may be used on the Platform. Similar technologies include web beacons (also known as pixel tags or clear GIFs), local storage objects, and device fingerprinting. Where we refer to "cookies" in this Cookie Policy, we intend to include all such similar tracking technologies unless the context indicates otherwise.

2. COOKIES WE USE

2.1 We categorise the cookies used on the Platform as follows:

(a) Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Platform to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually only set in response to actions made by you which amount to a request for services, such as logging in to your Account, setting your cookie preferences, or filling in forms. These cookies do not store any personally identifiable information and do not require your consent under Applicable Law.

(b) Analytics and Performance Cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of the Platform. They help us to know which pages are the most and least popular and see how visitors move around the Platform. All information collected by these cookies is aggregated and therefore anonymous. These cookies require your consent in jurisdictions where prior opt-in consent is mandated by Applicable Law, including the European Union under the ePrivacy Directive and the GDPR.

4.3 The information collected by GA4 cookies is transmitted to and stored by Google on servers in the United States. Google processes this data on our behalf as a data processor under our Data Processing Terms with Google. Google may also transfer this information to third parties where required by law or where such third parties process the information on Google's behalf.

4.4 We have configured Google Analytics to anonymise IP addresses (IP masking) before data is stored, so that no full IP address is retained. We do not use Google Analytics to collect or process any sensitive personal data.

4.5 You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by: (a) refusing consent for analytics cookies when prompted by our cookie consent banner; (b) installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on available at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout; or (c) configuring your browser to block third-party cookies.

4.6 For further information about Google's privacy practices and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, please visit https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

8. MANAGING COOKIES THROUGH YOUR BROWSER

8.1 In addition to the cookie preference centre on the Platform, you can control cookies through your web browser settings. Most browsers allow you to: (a) view the cookies stored on your device; (b) delete all or selected cookies; (c) block cookies from being set by all or specific websites; and (d) configure your browser to alert you when a cookie is being set.

8.2 Please note that blocking all cookies may affect the functionality of the Platform and other websites you visit. In particular, blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent you from logging in to your Account or using certain features of the Platform.

8.3 For instructions on how to manage cookies in your specific browser, please refer to:

Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and Other Site Data

Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data

Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy

Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions

Opera: Settings > Advanced > Privacy & Security > Cookies

8.4 You can also learn more about cookies and manage your preferences across multiple websites at www.aboutcookies.org or www.allaboutcookies.org.
