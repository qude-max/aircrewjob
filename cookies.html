1. WHAT ARE COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES?

1.1 Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) by a website when you visit it. They are widely used by website operators to make websites work efficiently, provide functionality, and collect information about how users interact with the site.

1.2 In addition to cookies, the Platform uses browser local storage — a similar client-side storage mechanism that saves data directly in your browser rather than transmitting it with every server request. Local storage data persists until it is cleared by you or expires by design.

1.3 Cookies may be set by us ("first-party") or by third parties providing services on our behalf, such as advertising networks ("third-party cookies"). Where we refer to "cookies" in this Cookie Policy, we intend to include all similar tracking technologies, including local storage, web beacons, and pixel tags, unless the context indicates otherwise.

1.4 Cookies may be "session cookies" (expire when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (remain for a set period). Both types may be in use on the Platform.

2. TECHNOLOGIES WE USE

2.1 We categorise the technologies used on the Platform as follows:

(a) Strictly Necessary

These are essential for the Platform to function. They enable core features such as logging in to your Account and storing your consent preferences. They do not require your prior consent under Applicable Law.

(b) Advertising and Conversion Tracking

We use Google Ads with Consent Mode v2 to measure conversion events — for example, tracking when a user clicks through to apply for a job as a result of seeing one of our advertisements. These technologies require your prior informed opt-in consent in jurisdictions where the GDPR and ePrivacy Directive apply. They are not used until consent is obtained.

4. VERCEL ANALYTICS

4.1 For general traffic statistics, we use Vercel Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics service provided by Vercel, Inc. as part of our hosting infrastructure. Unlike traditional analytics tools, Vercel Analytics does not use cookies or set any persistent identifiers on your device.

4.2 Vercel Analytics collects aggregated, anonymised data about page views and visitor counts derived from request-level data (such as the URL visited and approximate geographic region) without identifying individual users or tracking behaviour across sessions. No personal data is stored on your device in connection with Vercel Analytics.

4.3 Because Vercel Analytics does not use cookies or persistent identifiers, it does not require your consent under the ePrivacy Directive or the GDPR. It operates within Vercel's standard data processing practices. For further information, please see Vercel's privacy policy at https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

5. GOOGLE ADS AND CONSENT MODE V2

5.1 We use Google Ads conversion tracking, provided by Google LLC ("Google"), to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. Specifically, we track conversion events such as users clicking through to apply for a job as a result of an advertisement. Google Ads is deployed with Consent Mode v2, which means that advertising and conversion tracking cookies are only activated after you have given your explicit consent.

5.2 Where consent is not given, Google Ads Consent Mode v2 operates in a cookieless mode, using only aggregated and anonymised modelled data. No individual-level advertising cookie is set on your device without your consent.

5.3 Google Ads cookies transmit data to Google's servers, which may be located in the United States or other countries. Google processes this data on our behalf as a data processor under Google's advertising data processing terms. For further information about Google's data practices, please visit https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5.4 You can opt out of Google Ads conversion tracking at any time by: (a) withdrawing your consent through our cookie preference centre; (b) using Google's Ads Settings at https://adssettings.google.com; or (c) configuring your browser to block third-party cookies.

6. SUPABASE AND VERCEL (PLATFORM INFRASTRUCTURE)

6.1 The Platform uses Supabase for database and authentication services and Vercel for website hosting and content delivery. These services may process technical data in connection with the delivery of the Platform and the management of authenticated sessions.

6.2 Your authenticated session token is stored in your browser's local storage (not a cookie) under a Supabase-managed key. This is strictly necessary for the Platform to recognise you as a logged-in User. Without it, Account features will not function. You can clear this at any time through your browser's developer tools or privacy settings.

8. MANAGING COOKIES THROUGH YOUR BROWSER

8.1 You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, delete, and block cookies. You can also clear local storage through your browser's developer tools (typically under Application > Local Storage).

8.2 Blocking strictly necessary session storage may prevent you from logging in to your Account.

8.3 For browser-specific instructions:

Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and Other Site Data

Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data

Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy

Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions

Opera: Settings > Advanced > Privacy & Security > Cookies
