Cookies & local storage

Last updated: 10 June 2026 · Version 0.1 (draft)

⚠ Draft for legal review — accurate to how the site works today.

The short version

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and there's no cookie banner because none is needed. The platform uses only what's strictly necessary to function, which is why you've never been asked to "accept cookies" here.

What we actually store

  • Authentication token (localStorage) — set by Supabase when you sign in, so you stay signed in between visits. Strictly necessary; removed when you sign out.
  • Game scores & preferences (localStorage) — your aptitude-game personal bests live only in your browser and never reach our servers. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Analytics — we use Vercel Web Analytics, which is cookieless: it counts page views in aggregate without cookies, fingerprinting or cross-site tracking, and cannot identify you.

Third-party content

Some pages load assets from third parties (Google Fonts, map tiles from CARTO/OpenStreetMap, script CDNs, airline logos via a favicon service). These requests expose your IP address to those providers as a technical necessity, but we don't allow any of them to set tracking cookies on our pages. If you follow a link to Discord or an airline's careers site, their own cookie policies apply there.

Managing storage

You can clear localStorage anytime via your browser settings (usually under "Site data"). Doing so signs you out and resets game scores, nothing more.
