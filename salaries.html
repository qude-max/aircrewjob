US majors lead on raw numbers post-2023 contracts — but factor in training pace, commute and cost of living before comparing.

🏝️ Tax-free isn't free

Gulf packages look smaller on paper but include housing, schooling allowances and zero income tax — often beating European take-home.

📈 Command is the multiplier

The FO-to-captain jump is the single biggest pay event of your career. Airlines with fast upgrades (Wizz, Ryanair) can out-earn slower legacy paths.
