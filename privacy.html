PRIVACY POLICY

AIRCREWJOB.COM

Effective Date: 1 July 2026

Website: www.aircrewjob.com

This Privacy Policy describes how the operator of aircrewjob.com ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, discloses, and otherwise processes personal data in connection with your access to and use of the Platform at www.aircrewjob.com (the "Platform"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our Users and to handling personal data responsibly, transparently, and in compliance with all applicable data protection laws.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with our Terms and Conditions and Cookie Policy, both of which are published on the Platform.

1. IDENTITY OF THE DATA CONTROLLER

1.1 The data controller in respect of personal data processed through the Platform is the individual operator of aircrewjob.com, currently operating as an individual (not a registered company) from Singapore. If and when the operator incorporates a legal entity, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly. All enquiries relating to data protection should be directed to aircrewjobcontact@gmail.com.

1.2 For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and equivalent legislation in the United Kingdom, where applicable by virtue of our serving Users in those jurisdictions, we acknowledge our status as a data controller in respect of personal data processed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

1.3 For the purposes of the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 of Singapore ("PDPA"), as amended, we are an organisation responsible for personal data under our possession or control, including personal data to which we have given access to a data intermediary.

2. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY

2.1 This Privacy Policy applies to all personal data collected, used, disclosed, or otherwise processed by us in connection with the Platform, including personal data of: (a) Aircrew Users who register and create profiles on the Platform; (b) Recruiter Users who register to post job listings; (c) visitors who browse the Platform without registering; (d) individuals who subscribe to email job alerts; and (e) any other individuals who interact with us through the Platform.

2.2 This Privacy Policy does not apply to the personal data practices of third-party websites or services that may be linked to or integrated with the Platform, including without limitation Discord, Vercel, Google, Supabase, or Resend. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those third parties directly.

2.3 Where we receive personal data from Users located in the European Union, European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom, we will process such personal data in compliance with the GDPR, the UK GDPR, and any applicable national implementing legislation, in addition to the PDPA.

3. PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT

3.1 We collect personal data from you directly when you interact with the Platform. The categories of personal data we collect include:

(a) Registration and Account Data: your email address, username, and password (stored in hashed form) provided when you create an Account;

(b) Professional Credentials Data: your aviation licence number, licence type, total logged flight hours, type ratings, endorsements, and such other professional credentials as you choose to provide when completing your Aircrew profile;

(c) Profile Information: your name, professional biography, employment history, qualifications, profile photograph (if uploaded), and any other information you voluntarily include in your profile;

(d) Recruiter Data: company name, role, contact details, and job listing information provided by Recruiter Users;

(e) Job Application Data: records of jobs you have saved or applied to through the Platform;

(f) Email Alert Subscription Data: your email address and alert preference (pilot jobs, cabin crew jobs, or all jobs) if you sign up to receive email job alerts;

(g) Usage and Technical Data: information collected automatically when you access the Platform, including your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device identifiers, pages visited, referring URLs, and the date and time of your visits, as processed by Vercel's hosting and analytics infrastructure; and

(h) Advertising Interaction Data: conversion event data (such as clicking through to apply for a job as a result of seeing a Google Ads advertisement), collected through Google Ads Consent Mode v2, subject to your consent; and

(i) Correspondence Data: any personal data included in communications you send to us by email or through any other channel.

3.2 Note on planned features: in-Platform messaging between users, Recruiter browsing and search of Aircrew profiles, and flight school reviews and ratings are planned features not yet available on the Platform. This Privacy Policy will be updated when those features are introduced.

3.3 We do not currently collect payment card information directly. Should Premium Services be introduced, payment processing will be handled by a third-party payment processor and their applicable terms and privacy policy will apply to payment data.

3.4 We do not intentionally collect special categories of personal data as defined under the GDPR (such as health data, biometric data, racial or ethnic origin, or political opinions). Aviation medical examination details referenced in your profile are provided voluntarily and you consent to our processing them for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

4. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

4.1 Under the PDPA, we process your personal data on the basis of your consent, deemed consent, or as otherwise permitted by the PDPA, including for the purposes of fulfilling our contractual obligations to you and for legitimate interests consistent with the reasonable expectations of a person in your position.

4.2 Under the GDPR, where applicable, we rely on the following legal bases:

(a) Contract Performance (Article 6(1)(b)): processing necessary to provide the Services you have requested, including maintaining your Account, facilitating job applications, and enabling Recruiter access to job management features;

(b) Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)): operating and improving the Platform, ensuring security, preventing fraud, and managing our business, where such interests are not overridden by your privacy rights;

(c) Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): email job alert subscriptions, Google Ads conversion tracking, and any future direct marketing communications; and

(d) Legal Obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): compliance with applicable legal requirements.

4.3 Where processing is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

5. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

5.1 We collect and process your personal data for the following purposes:

(a) Account Management: to create, manage, and maintain your Account and to authenticate your identity;

(b) Service Delivery: to provide you with the Services described in our Terms and Conditions, including enabling Aircrew to create profiles and apply for jobs, and enabling Recruiters to post and manage listings;

(c) Email Job Alerts: to send you email notifications about new job postings matching your alert preference, where you have subscribed and consented;

(d) Platform Communications: to send you transactional and service notifications necessary for the operation of your Account;

(e) Safety and Security: to detect, investigate, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms and Conditions;

(f) Platform Analytics: to analyse usage patterns and improve the Platform using Vercel Analytics (cookieless, anonymised) and, with your consent, Google Ads conversion data;

(g) Advertising Measurement: to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns through Google Ads conversion tracking, subject to your consent;

(h) Legal Compliance: to comply with our obligations under Applicable Law; and

(i) Dispute Resolution: to investigate and resolve complaints and disputes arising in connection with the Platform.

6. EMAIL JOB ALERTS

6.1 You may subscribe to receive email job alerts from us by providing your email address and selecting your alert preference (pilot jobs, cabin crew jobs, or all jobs). Subscription to email alerts is entirely voluntary and is based on your consent.

6.2 Job alert emails are sent from alerts@aircrewjob.com. Replies and unsubscribe requests should be directed to aircrewjobcontact@gmail.com.

6.3 Email alert delivery is handled by Resend, Inc. ("Resend"), a third-party email delivery service. Resend processes your email address and delivery-related technical data on our behalf as a data processor. We have entered into a data processing agreement with Resend in accordance with our obligations under Applicable Law.

6.4 You may unsubscribe from job alert emails at any time by: (a) following the unsubscribe instructions included in each alert email; or (b) contacting us directly at aircrewjobcontact@gmail.com. Following an unsubscribe request, we will cease sending alert emails within a reasonable time and will retain your email address on a suppression list solely to ensure you are not re-added in error.

6.5 We will not use your email job alert subscription for any purpose other than sending you the job alerts you have requested.

7. RETENTION OF PERSONAL DATA

7.1 We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to satisfy any legal, regulatory, accounting, or reporting requirements.

7.2 General retention periods:

(a) Account and Profile Data: for the duration of your Account and up to three (3) years following deletion or termination;

(b) Job Application Data: up to two (2) years following the relevant application;

(c) Email Alert Subscription Data: for the duration of your subscription and, following unsubscribe, retained on a suppression list for up to two (2) years to prevent re-subscription in error;

(d) Usage and Technical Data: up to twelve (12) months, in line with Vercel Analytics' retention practices;

(e) Advertising Conversion Data: up to ninety (90) days, consistent with Google Ads' standard conversion data retention; and

(f) Legal and Compliance Data: for such longer period as required under Applicable Law or in connection with ongoing or anticipated legal proceedings.

7.3 When personal data is no longer required, we will securely delete or anonymise it.

8. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

8.1 Your personal data is stored on Supabase, Inc.'s infrastructure, which is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR-compliant. We have executed Supabase's Data Processing Addendum, incorporating Standard Contractual Clauses for international transfers. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest.

8.2 The Platform is hosted on Vercel, Inc.'s infrastructure, which provides enterprise-grade security including DDoS protection and edge encryption. We have executed Vercel's Data Processing Agreement.

8.3 We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures including access controls, encrypted transmission, and regular review of our data practices. No internet transmission is completely secure; you assume a degree of risk in transmitting data online.

8.4 In the event of a notifiable personal data breach, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commission of Singapore within three (3) business days (PDPA) and, where the GDPR applies, the relevant supervisory authority within seventy-two (72) hours.

9. DISCLOSURE OF PERSONAL DATA

9.1 We do not sell your personal data. We disclose personal data only in the circumstances below:

(a) Other Registered Users: Aircrew profiles are visible to other registered Users to the extent set by the Aircrew User's privacy settings. Recruiter job listings are visible to Aircrew Users. Note: full Recruiter search and browse of Aircrew profiles is a planned feature not yet available.

(b) Data Processors: we engage the following processors who process personal data on our behalf: (i) Supabase, Inc. (database and authentication); (ii) Vercel, Inc. (hosting and analytics); (iii) Google LLC (Google Ads conversion tracking); (iv) Resend, Inc. (email alert delivery); and (v) Discord, Inc. (community Discord server). Each is engaged under appropriate data processing agreements.

(c) Legal and Regulatory Authorities: where required by Applicable Law, court order, or lawful authority.

(d) Professional Advisers: legal counsel, accountants, and other advisers, subject to confidentiality obligations.

(e) Business Transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate data protection commitments.

10.1 The Platform is operated from Singapore. Your personal data may be transferred to or processed in the United States (where Supabase, Vercel, Google, Resend, and Discord are headquartered) and other countries.

10.2 For transfers from the EEA or UK to countries without an adequacy decision, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission and, for UK transfers, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

10.3 For transfers from Singapore to overseas organisations, we ensure those organisations are bound to provide a comparable standard of protection under the PDPA Transfer Limitation Obligation.

11.1 The Platform is intended for users who are at least 16 years of age. By using the Platform, you confirm that you are at least 16 years old.

11.2 We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under the age of 16. If we become aware that a user is under 16, we will take steps to delete that user's personal data promptly. If you believe a person under 16 has provided us with their personal data, please contact us at aircrewjobcontact@gmail.com.

11.3 Note: the Platform does not currently collect date of birth or ask users to verify their age at registration. The minimum age statement above is a terms-based restriction. Quentin, please consider whether you wish to introduce an age declaration checkbox at sign-up as the platform grows.

(b) Right to Correction: to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;

(c) Right to Erasure (GDPR): to request deletion in certain circumstances;
