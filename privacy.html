PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: 1 July 2026

Website: www.aircrewjob.com

This Privacy Policy describes how the operator of aircrewjob.com ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, discloses, and otherwise processes personal data in connection with your access to and use of the Platform at www.aircrewjob.com (the "Platform"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our Users and to handling personal data responsibly, transparently, and in compliance with all applicable data protection laws.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with our Terms and Conditions and Cookie Policy, both of which are published on the Platform.

1. IDENTITY OF THE DATA CONTROLLER

1.1 The data controller in respect of personal data processed through the Platform is the individual operator of aircrewjob.com, operating from Singapore. As at the Effective Date, the Platform does not operate under a formally registered company name. All enquiries relating to data protection should be directed through the contact details set out in Section 25 of this Privacy Policy.

1.2 For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and equivalent legislation in the United Kingdom, where applicable by virtue of our serving Users in those jurisdictions, we acknowledge our status as a data controller in respect of personal data processed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

1.3 For the purposes of the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 of Singapore ("PDPA"), as amended, we are an organisation responsible for personal data under our possession or control, including personal data to which we have given access to a data intermediary.

2. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY

2.1 This Privacy Policy applies to all personal data collected, used, disclosed, or otherwise processed by us in connection with the Platform, including personal data of: (a) Aircrew Users who register and create profiles on the Platform; (b) Recruiter Users who register to access Aircrew profiles and post job listings; (c) visitors who browse the Platform without registering; and (d) any other individuals who interact with us through the Platform.

2.2 This Privacy Policy does not apply to the personal data practices of third-party websites or services that may be linked to or integrated with the Platform, including without limitation Discord, Vercel, or Google Analytics. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those third parties directly.

2.3 Where we receive personal data from Users located in the European Union, European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom, we will process such personal data in compliance with the GDPR, the UK GDPR, and any applicable national implementing legislation, in addition to the PDPA.

3. PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT

3.1 We collect personal data from you directly when you interact with the Platform. The categories of personal data we collect include:

(a) Registration and Account Data: your email address, username, and password (stored in hashed form) provided when you create an Account;

(b) Professional Credentials Data: your aviation licence number, licence type, total logged flight hours, type ratings, endorsements, and such other professional credentials as you choose to provide when completing your Aircrew profile;

(c) Profile Information: your name, professional biography, employment history, qualifications, profile photograph (if uploaded), and any other information you voluntarily include in your profile;

(d) Recruiter Data: company name, role, contact details, and job listing information provided by Recruiter Users;

(e) Communications Data: messages, enquiries, and communications you send to other Users through the Platform's messaging features or to us directly;

(f) Reviews and Ratings: the content of any reviews or ratings you submit about flight schools listed on the Platform;

(g) Usage and Technical Data: information collected automatically when you access the Platform, including your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device identifiers, pages visited, referring URLs, and the date and time of your visits, as collected through Vercel's hosting infrastructure and Google Analytics; and

(h) Correspondence Data: any personal data included in communications you send to us by email, through the Platform's contact form, or through any other channel.

3.2 We do not currently collect payment card information directly. Should Premium Services be introduced, payment processing will be handled by a third-party payment processor and their applicable terms and privacy policy will apply to payment data.

3.3 We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data as defined under the GDPR (also referred to as special categories of personal data), such as racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, trade union membership, biometric data, genetic data, health data, or sexual orientation. However, we note that aviation medical examination details or references to aviation medical examiners in your profile could touch on health-related information. If you choose to include any such information, you do so voluntarily and you consent to our processing of that information for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

4. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

4.1 Under the PDPA, we process your personal data on the basis of your consent, deemed consent, or as otherwise permitted by the PDPA, including for the purposes of fulfilling our contractual obligations to you and for legitimate interests consistent with the reasonable expectations of a person in your position.

4.2 Under the GDPR, where applicable, we rely on the following legal bases for processing your personal data:

(a) Contract Performance (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): processing is necessary for the performance of the contract constituted by our Terms and Conditions and to provide you with the Services you request, including maintaining your Account, facilitating job applications, and enabling Recruiter access to Aircrew profiles;

(b) Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): we process certain personal data on the basis of our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Platform, ensuring security, preventing fraud, and managing our business, where such interests are not overridden by your privacy interests or fundamental rights and freedoms;

(c) Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR): where we send you direct marketing communications or use non-essential cookies and tracking technologies, we will rely on your freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent; and

(d) Legal Obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR): we may process personal data where required to do so in order to comply with a legal obligation to which we are subject.

4.3 Where our processing of your personal data is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent shall not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

5. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

5.1 We collect and process your personal data for the following purposes:

(a) Account Management: to create, manage, verify, and maintain your Account and to authenticate your identity when you log in to the Platform;

(b) Service Delivery: to provide you with the Services described in our Terms and Conditions, including enabling Aircrew to create profiles and apply for jobs, and enabling Recruiters to search for and contact Aircrew;

(c) Platform Communications: to send you notifications, service updates, transactional emails, and other communications necessary for the operation of your Account and the Platform;

(d) Safety and Security: to detect, investigate, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our Terms and Conditions;

(e) Platform Improvement: to analyse usage patterns, diagnose technical issues, and improve the functionality, performance, and user experience of the Platform, including through the use of Google Analytics;

(f) Legal Compliance: to comply with our obligations under Applicable Law, including responding to lawful requests from governmental or regulatory authorities;

(g) Dispute Resolution: to investigate and resolve complaints, disputes, and claims arising in connection with the Platform; and

(h) Marketing and Promotional Communications: where you have given your consent or where otherwise permitted by Applicable Law, to send you marketing communications about new features, updates, or developments relating to the Platform.

6. RETENTION OF PERSONAL DATA

6.1 We retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, regulatory, accounting, or reporting requirements.

6.2 As a general guide, we apply the following retention periods, which may be extended or reduced in accordance with our legal obligations or legitimate interests:

(a) Account and Profile Data: retained for the duration of your Account and for a period of up to three (3) years following account deletion or termination, to allow for the resolution of any outstanding disputes and to comply with applicable legal obligations;

(b) Job Applications and Recruiter Interaction Data: retained for a period of up to two (2) years following the relevant application or interaction;

(c) Communications and Correspondence Data: retained for a period of up to three (3) years;

(d) Usage and Technical Data: retained for a period of up to twenty-six (26) months, consistent with Google Analytics' default data retention period, and otherwise in accordance with applicable technical data retention standards; and

(e) Legal and Compliance Data: retained for such longer period as may be required under Applicable Law or in connection with ongoing or anticipated legal proceedings.

6.3 When personal data is no longer required, we will securely delete or anonymise it in accordance with our internal data destruction procedures and Applicable Law.

7. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

7.1 Your personal data is stored on Supabase, a third-party database and backend infrastructure provider. Supabase processes data on our behalf as a data processor, pursuant to a Data Processing Agreement. Supabase is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR-compliant, and employs industry-standard encryption for data at rest and in transit. We have selected Supabase's infrastructure region in accordance with applicable data residency considerations.

7.2 We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure, or access. These measures include: (a) encryption of personal data in transit using TLS/SSL protocols; (b) access controls restricting access to personal data to authorised personnel with a need-to-know; (c) regular review of our data processing practices and security measures; and (d) our hosting on Vercel, which provides enterprise-grade hosting infrastructure with built-in security features.

7.3 Notwithstanding the foregoing, you acknowledge that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your personal data and you assume a degree of risk in transmitting personal data over the internet.

7.4 In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commission of Singapore and, where the GDPR applies, the relevant supervisory authority, within the timeframes required under Applicable Law. Where required by law, we will also notify affected individuals without undue delay.

8. DISCLOSURE OF PERSONAL DATA

8.1 We do not sell your personal data to any third party. We will not share your personal data with third parties except in the circumstances set out in this Section 8.

8.2 We may disclose your personal data to the following categories of recipients:

(a) Other Registered Users: Aircrew profiles and professional credential information are visible to Recruiter Users registered on the Platform, and to other Aircrew Users to the extent determined by the Aircrew User's privacy settings. Recruiters' company details and job listings are visible to Aircrew Users;

(b) Data Processors and Sub-processors: we engage the following data processors who process personal data on our behalf: (i) Supabase, Inc. (database hosting and backend services); (ii) Vercel, Inc. (website hosting and content delivery); and (iii) Google LLC (analytics services through Google Analytics 4). Each of these processors has been engaged under appropriate data processing agreements and is required to process personal data only on our instructions and in compliance with Applicable Law;

(c) Discord Inc.: where you access or participate in the Platform-linked Discord server, certain identifying information may be disclosed to or processed by Discord Inc. in accordance with Discord's own Privacy Policy;

(d) Legal and Regulatory Authorities: we may disclose personal data to governmental, regulatory, or law enforcement authorities where required to do so by Applicable Law, court order, or lawful authority, or where we believe in good faith that such disclosure is necessary to protect our legal rights, prevent fraud, or protect the safety of our Users or the public;

(e) Professional Advisers: we may disclose personal data to our legal counsel, accountants, or other professional advisers where necessary for the provision of their services to us, subject to confidentiality obligations; and

(f) Business Transfers: in the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, sale of assets, or similar corporate transaction, we may transfer personal data to the relevant successor or acquiring entity, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data protection commitments.

9. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

9.1 The Platform is operated from Singapore. Your personal data may be transferred to, stored in, or processed in countries other than your country of residence, including the United States (where Supabase, Vercel, Google, and Discord are headquartered) and any country in which data centres operated by our processors are located.

9.2 Where we transfer personal data originating from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or any other jurisdiction that imposes transfer restrictions to a country that does not provide an equivalent level of data protection, we will implement appropriate safeguards, which may include: (a) Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission; (b) the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or UK Addendum; (c) an adequacy decision; or (d) any other appropriate transfer mechanism recognised under Applicable Law.

9.3 For transfers from Singapore to overseas organisations, we will ensure that such organisations are bound by legally enforceable obligations to provide a standard of protection to the transferred personal data that is at least comparable to the protection afforded under the PDPA, including by entering into contractual arrangements in accordance with the PDPA Transfer Limitation Obligation.

10. CHILDREN'S PERSONAL DATA

10.1 In accordance with the Advisory Guidelines on the PDPA for Children's Personal Data in the Digital Environment issued by the Personal Data Protection Commission of Singapore on 28 March 2024 ("Children's Data Guidelines"), we apply enhanced protections to the personal data of individuals under the age of eighteen (18).

10.2 We apply the following age-specific consent standards: (a) for Users under the age of thirteen (13), we require verifiable parental or guardian consent before any registration or collection of personal data, and parents or guardians must be notified of all purposes for which the child's personal data will be collected, used, or disclosed; and (b) for Users between the ages of thirteen (13) and seventeen (17), we may obtain consent directly from the User, provided that we have no reason to believe that the User lacks sufficient understanding of the nature and consequences of providing consent, and that the consent mechanism is clearly communicated and easily withdrawable.

10.3 We endeavour to use age-appropriate language and presentation in communicating with younger Users regarding the collection and use of their personal data. Parents or guardians of a minor User may exercise data subject rights on behalf of the minor User by contacting us through the details in Section 25.

10.4 We do not knowingly use the personal data of Users under the age of eighteen (18) for any purpose other than the delivery of the Services in a manner appropriate to their age and the consent provided.

11.1 We use cookies and similar tracking technologies on the Platform. A full description of the cookies used, their purposes, and your consent options is set out in our Cookie Policy, which is published at www.aircrewjob.com/cookie-policy and incorporated by reference into this Privacy Policy.

Google Analytics sets cookies including the _ga and _gid cookies. These cookies are classified as non-essential analytics cookies and are subject to your consent in jurisdictions where prior consent is required under the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive.

11.3 You may manage your cookie preferences through the cookie consent mechanism available on the Platform. Withdrawing consent for non-essential cookies will not affect the functionality of essential Platform features.

12. YOUR RIGHTS AS A DATA SUBJECT

12.1 Subject to Applicable Law and any applicable exemptions, you have the following rights in respect of your personal data:

(a) Right of Access: you have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and information about how we process it;

(b) Right to Correction: you have the right to request that we correct any personal data about you that is inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading;

(c) Right to Erasure (GDPR): where the GDPR applies, you have the right to request the deletion of your personal data in certain circumstances, including where the data is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, where you withdraw consent, or where you object to processing and there are no overriding legitimate grounds;

(d) Right to Restrict Processing (GDPR): where the GDPR applies, you have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances;

(e) Right to Data Portability (GDPR): where the GDPR applies and processing is based on consent or contract and carried out by automated means, you have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and to transmit that data to another controller;

(f) Right to Object (GDPR): where the GDPR applies, you have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes;

(g) Rights in Relation to Automated Decision-Making (GDPR): where the GDPR applies, you have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects you, except in limited circumstances; and

(h) Right to Withdraw Consent: where processing is based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing conducted prior to such withdrawal.

12.2 To exercise any of the rights described in this Section 12, please contact us using the details set out in Section 25. We will respond to your request within thirty (30) days of receipt, or within the timeframe required under Applicable Law. We may require verification of your identity before processing your request.

12.3 If you are dissatisfied with our response to a data subject rights request or believe we have processed your personal data unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Commission of Singapore (www.pdpc.gov.sg) or, where the GDPR applies, with the competent data protection supervisory authority in your country of residence.

13.1 As at the Effective Date, we do not engage in automated decision-making that produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects individuals. We do not use profiling to make automated decisions about Users' employment suitability, creditworthiness, or other significant personal matters.

13.2 Aggregated and anonymised analytics data generated through Google Analytics is used solely to understand Platform usage trends and improve the Services. This analytics data is not used to make individual decisions about Users.

13.3 If we introduce any automated decision-making process that produces legal or similarly significant effects, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly and, where required by the GDPR, provide you with the information required by Article 22 GDPR.

16. SECURITY INCIDENT AND BREACH NOTIFICATION

16.1 In the event of a personal data breach, we will conduct a prompt internal investigation and assessment. Where the breach is notifiable under the PDPA (i.e., where it is likely to result in significant harm to the affected individuals or affects ten (10) or more individuals), we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commission within three (3) business days of our assessment that the breach is notifiable.

16.2 Where the GDPR applies and the breach is likely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals, we will notify the competent supervisory authority within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of the breach. Where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to individuals, we will also notify affected data subjects without undue delay.

16.3 We maintain a data breach response plan and conduct regular reviews of our security measures to minimise the likelihood and impact of security incidents.
