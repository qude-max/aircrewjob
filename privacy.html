Privacy & Data Protection

Last updated: 10 June 2026 · Version 0.1 (draft)

⚠ Draft for legal review. This policy reflects how the platform actually works today, but it has not yet been validated by a qualified lawyer. Do not treat it as final legal text.

Who we are

AirCrew Jobs ("we", "us") operates aircrewjob.com, a careers platform for pilots and (soon) cabin crew. For data-protection purposes the data controller is the operator of AirCrew Jobs. Contact: aircrewjobcontact@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

  • Account data — email address, name, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), and your chosen role (pilot, cabin crew or recruiter). Recruiters additionally provide a company name. Purpose: operating your account. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • Pilot profile data — licence type, total flying hours, type ratings, and job-alert preferences, all provided voluntarily by you. Purpose: pre-filling applications and matching vacancies. Legal basis: consent; you may leave these fields empty.
  • Application data — when you apply to a vacancy posted by a recruiter on our platform, your name, email, licence, hours, ratings and cover note are shared with that recruiter only. Purpose: recruitment. Legal basis: performance of a contract at your request.
  • Saved jobs and game scores — stored to provide the feature. Aptitude-game scores are stored only in your browser (localStorage) and never reach our servers.
  • Technical data — if analytics are enabled, we use privacy-focused, aggregate page analytics (no cross-site tracking, no advertising profiles).

We do not sell personal data, run advertising, or use third-party tracking cookies.

Where "Apply on airline site" leads

Most listings link directly to the airline's own careers portal. Anything you submit there is governed by that airline's privacy policy, not ours — we never see those applications.

Who processes data on our behalf

  • Supabase — database and authentication hosting (account, profile and application data).
  • Vercel — website hosting and, if enabled, aggregate analytics.
  • Google Fonts & favicon service, CARTO/OpenStreetMap map tiles, jsDelivr/unpkg CDNs — these load assets when pages render, which technically exposes your IP address to those providers, as on most websites.
  • Discord — our community ("Chill Wings") is an external service governed by Discord's own terms and privacy policy.

How long we keep data

Account, profile and application data are kept while your account exists. Delete your account (or ask us to) and the associated profile, saved jobs and applications are removed. Recruiters' copies of applications you already sent them are retained per the recruitment relationship you entered.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EU/UK GDPR and Singapore's PDPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, and to complain to your data-protection authority. To exercise any of these, email aircrewjobcontact@gmail.com — we aim to respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our hosting providers. Access to application data is restricted by row-level database security: pilots see only their own applications; recruiters see only applications to their own listings; only platform administrators can see more.

Children

The platform is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We'll update this page as the platform evolves and bump the version number above. Material changes will be announced on the site.
